How this place works

Each place can contain other places, including notes and practices. Only the center, its children, and their children appear at once. Editing and repetitions save locally. Export preserves the entire garden, including the current view; loading replaces this browser’s garden after confirmation.

Imported gardens describe behavior; they cannot execute JavaScript. A bot can use the same buttons or window.zenGarden.inspect() and window.zenGarden.dispatch(action).
